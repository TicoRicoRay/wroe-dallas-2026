How the Sponsor Contest Works

When your invitees register, they're asked who referred them. Make sure they type your company name, spelling counts, since it's not a dropdown. The sponsor who brings the most attendees gets bragging rights and a trophy during the Awards + Happy Hour celebration at the end of the day.

Best Practices

  • Tag people, don't just name them. Tagged people get notified, and their networks amplify the post several times over.
  • Post from your personal profile for the warmest engagement. Use your business page for the professional variant.
  • Best time to post: weekday mornings (7:30 to 10 AM CT) or Sunday evenings (6 to 8 PM CT).
  • Reply to every comment in the first 3 hours, early engagement signals quality to the algorithm.
  • For LinkedIn: put the registration link in the first comment, not the post body, since LinkedIn suppresses posts with external links.
