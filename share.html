Best Practices
  • Tag people, don't just name them. Tagged people get notified — their networks amplify the post 5–10×.
  • Post from your personal profile for warmest engagement. Use your business Page for the professional variant.
  • Best time to post: Weekday mornings (7:30–10 AM CT) or Sunday evenings (6–8 PM CT).
  • Reply to every comment in the first 3 hours — the algorithm treats early engagement as quality.
  • For LinkedIn: put the URL in the first comment, not the post body — LinkedIn suppresses posts with external links.
